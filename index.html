ABOUT ME

Building Intelligent Solutions Through Artificial Intelligence & Data Science

I am an enthusiastic Data Science and Artificial Intelligence student with a passion for solving real-world problems through Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and AI-powered automation. I enjoy transforming raw data into meaningful insights that help businesses make smarter decisions.

My technical skills include Python, NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, Keras, and data visualization. I continuously improve my knowledge by building AI projects, predictive models, and automation solutions while learning modern AI technologies and Prompt Engineering.

Before pursuing Artificial Intelligence, I gained professional experience in Sales, Real Estate, Property Consulting, and Car Insurance. This background strengthened my communication, client management, and business problem-solving skills, allowing me to combine technical expertise with practical business knowledge.

I also work as a Graphic Designer and Video Editor, giving me a creative perspective when designing user-focused digital experiences. I am currently based in Manila, Philippines and am open to both remote and on-site opportunities.
